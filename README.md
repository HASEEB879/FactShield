## 🌍 Overview

FactShield is an AI-powered platform designed to help people verify claims, news, images, and online content before trusting or sharing them.

Our goal is to make fact-checking fast, transparent, and accessible by combining artificial intelligence with trusted evidence and reliable sources.

---

## ✨ Vision

Build one of the world's most trusted AI fact-checking platforms.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Provide everyone with free and accessible tools to combat misinformation through explainable AI and trusted citations.
